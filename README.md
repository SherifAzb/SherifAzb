### 🧪 Origin Story

Every hero has an origin. Mine was a **10,000-row Excel sheet** full of nulls, broken date formats, and zero documentation — and I made it readable.

Since then, I've been on a mission: turn raw, chaotic data into dashboards and insights that actually drive decisions. I went from cleaning that sheet to writing my **own RFM segmentation model in Python** — because eventually the dashboards weren't enough, I needed to understand what was happening *underneath* them.

Currently in my **2nd year** of **Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence** at Benha University (`GPA 3.77/4.00`), self-training across Python, Power BI, ETL, and data modeling — one industry-style project at a time.

### 📁 Mission Log — Capstone Projects

<details open>
<summary><b>🎯 RFM Customer Segmentation — Python · Pandas</b></summary>
<br>

Built a Recency-Frequency-Monetary (RFM) model from scratch in Python on an online retail transaction dataset to score and segment customers — no BI tool, just raw pandas logic.

Classified customers into actionable groups (**Champions, At Risk, Hibernating**) to support targeted retention and marketing strategy.

`Python` `Pandas` `RFM Modeling` `Customer Segmentation`
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📡 Vodafone Qatar — Customer Churn Analysis</b></summary>
<br>

Cleaned unstructured raw datasets with Power Query and built a star schema model to optimize dashboard performance.

Identified a **26.5% churn rate** and the key drop-off factors, enabling a targeted retention strategy for executive stakeholders.

`Power Query` `Star Schema` `ETL` `DAX`
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📱 Samsung — Executive Dashboard & Supply Chain Report</b></summary>
<br>

Built an executive Power BI report tracking **$176.95M in revenue** (FY 2023–2024) across regions and product categories.

Designed a star schema and custom DAX measures (Total Sales, Unit Margin %, Top-Selling Series) to support supply chain and inventory decisions.

`DAX` `Star Schema` `Decomposition Trees`
</details>

`Power BI` `Star Schema` `DAX`
